Sounds used by the Repertoire practice band (cut, level-matched and compressed to MP3 for this app)
=====================================================================================================

PIANO  (piano.mp3)
  Salamander Grand Piano V3 — recorded by Alexander Holm; SFZ remap by kinwie.
  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SalamanderGrandPiano  |  https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0) — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
  Changes: notes D#2 to A5 (the whole range the app can play, with margin) in 5 velocity layers (v5, v8, v10, v12, v15), trimmed, kept in stereo (AB microphone pair), MP3. Text: LICENSE-Salamander-CC-BY-3.0.txt

ELECTRIC PIANO  (ep.mp3)
  jRhodes3d — 1977 Rhodes Mark I Stage 73 sampled by Jeremy Learman.
  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/jlearman.jRhodes3d
  Licence for the samples: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 (CC BY-NC 4.0).
  >>> NON-COMMERCIAL USE ONLY. If this app is ever sold or monetised, remove ep.mp3 (the app then falls
  >>> back to its built-in synth) or ask the author for a commercial licence. Text: LICENSE-jRhodes3d.txt
  Changes: the stereo version (jRhodes3d-st), 3 velocity layers over the range the app uses, trimmed, EQ (+3 dB low shelf at 250 Hz, -4 dB high shelf at 3.5 kHz) to soften the recording's treble boost, layer levels kept as recorded, MP3.

DRUMS  (kit_rusty.mp3, kit_rustybrush.mp3)
  Big Rusty Drums — Karoryfer Samples. https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.big-rusty-drums
  Licence: CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication). Credit given as a courtesy. Text: LICENSE-BigRustyDrums-CC0.txt
  Changes: hits with 3 velocity layers and several round-robins per piece (close + overhead mics mixed), mono, MP3.

UPRIGHT BASS  (bass_upright.mp3)
  Meatbass — 1958 Otto Rubner double bass, pizzicato. Karoryfer Samples / Drogomir Smolken.
  https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.meatbass — CC0 1.0 (royalty-free).

ELECTRIC BASSES  (bass_finger.mp3, bass_pick.mp3, bass_slap.mp3, bass_dub.mp3)
  Black and Blue Basses — Karoryfer Samples. https://github.com/sfzinstruments/karoryfer.black-and-blue-basses
  CC0 1.0. Changes: selected notes, high-pass below each note's fundamental (removes rumble), presence EQ and light saturation so the bass is heard on small speakers, mono, MP3 (the dub bass is additionally low-pass filtered at 1.5 kHz).

ORGAN  (organ.mp3)
  FluidR3_GM SoundFont — Copyright (c) 2000-2002, 2008 Frank Wen — MIT licence. Text: LICENSE-FluidR3.txt
